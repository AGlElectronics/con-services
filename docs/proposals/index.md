# Proposals

Proposals are design decisions that are not built yet. They record the
problem, the chosen shape, and what Con Services will deliberately *not*
build, so later work has something to follow.

| Proposal | Status | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Using the convention's existing identity provider](existing-identity-provider.md) | Agreed, not implemented | Reuse a convention's existing staff and attendee realms (or any OIDC IdP) without moving accounts: attendee reuse, brokered and direct staff modes, HR link-only mode, volunteer review queue and spreadsheet importer |
| [Staff communications](staff-communications.md) | Draft | Split notices, chat and meetings; build Herald for notices and meeting cards; keep Telegram/Discord (and similar) external |
