# Learn for learners

This guide is for everyone who takes courses in Learn: volunteers, crew and
staff. You need no special role; a trainer enrols you, your team's group
enrols you automatically, or you enrol yourself from the catalog.

**Words used here**

- **Course**: a set of pages, quizzes and in-person sessions.
- **Enrolment**: being on a course. It is what lets you open it.
- **Qualification**: what finishing a course certifies, for example
  *First aid*. You get a **certificate** for it.
- **Cycle**: one round through a course. When a qualification expires you
  renew it by going through the course again, in a new cycle.

## Find your courses

1. Sign in at your Learn address (for example
   `https://learn.example.org`) with your organisation account.
2. **My courses** lists every course you are on, with your progress and due
   date. Overdue courses are marked.
3. To join a course yourself, open the **catalog** on the same page and
   choose **Enrol**. Only courses that allow self-enrolment are listed.

You see: the course appears under **My courses** with `0/N done`.

## Work through a course

1. Open the course. Pages, quizzes and sessions are listed by section.
2. Read a page and mark it **done**. A lock means another activity has to be
   finished first; the lock says which.
3. Take a quiz: start it, answer every question and submit. You see your
   score, which answers were right and the feedback. A quiz counts as done
   when you reach its pass mark. The quiz shows how many attempts you have
   left.
4. When every activity is done and the course awards a qualification, your
   certificate is issued at once.

## In-person sessions and check-in

A **session** is a training at the venue, with one or more time slots.

1. Open the session in the course and **Book** a slot. When it is full you
   can **Join waitlist**; you move up automatically when a seat frees up.
   You can cancel until the slot starts.
2. At the venue, the trainer shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone: Learn
   opens and checks you in. If scanning doesn't work, open the link from the
   session page and type the **code shown under the QR**, then **Check in**.
3. You see **You are checked in**. Attending completes the session.

The code changes every 30 seconds, so scan the one on the screen now.
Without a booking you are checked in as a walk-in while seats are left.

## Certificates

1. **My qualifications** (on My courses) lists your certificates with their
   expiry date.
2. Open **Certificate** to see or **Print** it. It carries a QR code anyone
   can scan to check it is real, at `/verify/<code>`; the check shows only
   your name, the qualification, the dates and whether it is valid.
3. When a certificate is about to expire, **Renew** starts a new cycle of the
   course. Finish it again to get a new certificate; the old one stays in
   your history.

## Reminders

Learn reminds you of due courses, sessions tomorrow and expiring
certificates by email. To get them on Telegram as well:

1. Start a chat with your organisation's bot in Telegram and send `/start`.
2. Find your chat id (for example by sending a message to `@userinfobot`).
3. In Learn, **My courses → Notifications**, enter it under **Telegram chat
   id** and save.

## Your data

Learn keeps your name, email, progress, quiz attempts, attendance and
certificates. Someone who hasn't used Learn for a long time (36 months by
default; your organisation may set another period) is anonymised
automatically. You can ask your organisation to erase you sooner. Your
certificates keep working after that, with the name "Former learner".

## Troubleshooting

- **A course I expect is missing.** You are not enrolled yet, the course is
  not published, or it was archived. Ask the trainer.
- **"You are not enrolled in this course".** Ask the trainer to enrol you,
  or enrol yourself from the catalog if the course allows it.
- **A page or quiz is locked.** Finish the activities the lock names first.
- **"You have used all your attempts on this quiz".** Ask the trainer; only
  a course manager can reset a quiz.
- **"This check-in code is not valid any more".** The code changed; scan the
  QR code on the screen again.
- **Check-in says the session is not open.** Check-in opens 30 minutes
  before the start and closes at the end.
- **"Too many requests".** Wait a minute and try again.
